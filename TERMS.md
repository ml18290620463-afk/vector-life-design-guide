### 3. AI output disclaimer (read this)

Morning Star analyses are produced by large language models. They:

- Are **not** medical, mental-health, legal, financial, investment,

By clicking "ENGRAVE" / "REFLECT" you acknowledge the above and accept
that the output is informational only.

### 3a. Memoir (心象) creations

The "Memoir" feature lets you create an AI persona grounded in **your own
memory** of a real person (a relative, friend, mentor, or a past version
of yourself).

- A Memoir is a **creative interpretation** authored by you, made tangible
  by AI. It is **not** the real person; it makes no claim to represent
  the real person's actual views, feelings, or current state.
- You are responsible for ensuring you have a meaningful basis to create
  a Memoir of the person involved (e.g. they are a close relation, the
  Memoir is for private personal use, the content does not defame or
  expose the real person). You agree not to use the Memoir feature to:
  - Create personas of public figures, celebrities, or strangers in ways
    that could be confused with the real person.
  - Embed third-party private contact information (phone numbers,
    addresses, ID numbers) into the Memoir's description or memories.
  - Generate or distribute content that could be reasonably perceived as
    speech or actions of the real person.
- Memoir content (description, system prompt, accumulated memories) and
  conversation transcripts are stored **locally on your device** and pass
  through our AI proxy only at chat / extraction time. See
  [`PRIVACY.md`](./PRIVACY.md) §3a.
- Memoir conversations are an **emotional companion experience, not a
  substitute for professional grief, mental-health, or bereavement
  support**. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust
  or a regional crisis hotline.

By creating a Memoir you tick a consent checkbox confirming you understand
the above. We may suspend Memoir access for accounts that violate the
acceptable-use restrictions in this section or in §4.

### 3b. Letter Mode (写信模式)

The "Write a letter" feature lets you compose a delayed message to a
Memoir. The reply is generated by AI when the delivery window opens.

- The user-facing **delay is intentional**: a Memoir's reply is **not
  evidence** that anyone is "thinking of you" or "responding in real
  time". It is a deferred AI generation, scheduled by your local
  device.
- The reply is written in the Memoir's voice **as you described it**.
  It carries no information about the real person beyond what your
  letter and the Memoir's accumulated memories already encode.
- Letter Mode is **not** appropriate for messages you would send to
  the real person if they were alive / reachable. If you want to
  reach a real human, use a real channel.

### 3c. Echo Chamber (圆桌)

The "Round table" feature asks 3-7 personas to weigh in on a single
question and surfaces both consensus AND disagreement.

- The disagreement section is **a feature, not a flaw**. The point
  of consulting many voices is to get more than one answer; the
  synthesis deliberately resists rounding the personas toward a
  polite middle ground.
- The advice produced by a round table is still **AI-generated text**
  and is subject to the same caveats as §3 — it is informational,
  not medical / mental-health / legal / financial / career advice.
- Round-table responses including a Memoir voice carry the same
  「这位 X 的记忆中说过的话」 framing as the standalone Memoir
  surface (§3a). The persona is a creative interpretation grounded
  in YOUR memory of the person, not the person themselves.

### 3d. Cross-device migration (Phase 4.5 §E)

The cross-device migration wizard helps you carry your full vault
between your own devices.

- The wizard's `.vectormigration` file contains everything you've
  accumulated in VECTOR: journal entries, custom personas / Memoirs,
  Memoir long-term memories, pending Letter Mode letters, and
  optionally the master password hash + salt. Treat this file as
  **the most sensitive single artifact VECTOR can produce** —
  store and transmit it accordingly.
- The 6-character verification code shown by the wizard is an
  informational checksum, NOT a cryptographic signature. It helps
  you spot accidental corruption or "wrong file picked", but does
  NOT defend against an attacker who can also alter the displayed
  code. Use trusted physical channels (USB, AirDrop) when the
  attacker model includes a hostile network.
- VECTOR does not run a migration relay server. The wizard never
  uploads your file. Whatever channel you use to move the file
  between devices (cloud drive, email, etc.) is **your** channel —
  read its provider's privacy policy.
- If you import the credential snapshot, the new device acquires
  the **same** master password as the old device. Do not use the
  credential carry option when the new device will belong to a
  different person; uncheck the "Include my master password" box
  in the export modal in that case.

### 3e. Backup integrity (Phase 4 §4.b-3 — Ed25519 signatures)

VECTOR signs every migration package it produces with a per-device
Ed25519 keypair, and verifies signatures on every package it
imports. This upgrades the §3d "checksum is not a signature"
caveat into a real cryptographic guarantee.

- The first time you set a master password, VECTOR generates an
  Ed25519 keypair locally. The PUBLIC key is shown in Settings as
  a 16-character fingerprint (e.g. `ABCD-EFGH-IJKL-MNOP`); the
  SECRET key never leaves your device, and is encrypted at rest
  under the master password.
- The migration export modal shows the same fingerprint after
  generating a signed package. The migration import wizard, on
  the receiving device, displays this fingerprint and asks you
  to confirm "is this your other device?" — only on confirmation
  does the wizard apply the import. Subsequent imports from the
  same source device skip the prompt (TOFU — trust on first use).
- An invalid signature **hard-blocks** the import; this means a
  bit was flipped in transit, OR an attacker tried to substitute
  a forged file. Either way, ask your source device to re-export
  rather than ignoring the error.
- "Regenerate device keys" in Settings discards the current
  keypair and mints a new one. Do this if you suspect compromise
  of the local IndexedDB (e.g. malware on the device) or before
  passing the device to someone else. Receiving devices that
  trusted the old key will see the new key as "unknown" and
  re-trigger the TOFU prompt.

### 3a. 心象（Memoir）

"心象" 功能允许你基于**自己的记忆**为真实存在过的人（亲人、朋友、导师，或
过去的自己）创建 AI 人格。

- 心象是**你创作的诠释**，借助 AI 具象化。它**不是**那个真实的人；它不
  代表真实人物的现实观点、情感或当下状态。
- 你需对创建心象的依据负责（例如：与该人物有密切关系、心象仅供个人私下使用、
  内容不会诽谤或暴露真实人物）。你同意不用心象功能：
  - 创建可能与公众人物、明星或陌生人本身产生混淆的人格；
  - 在心象的描述或记忆中嵌入他人的私人联系信息（电话、地址、身份证号）；
  - 生成或传播可能被合理认为是真实人物本人言论或行为的内容。
- 心象的内容（描述、system prompt、积累的记忆）与对话记录**仅本地存储于你的
  设备**，仅在对话/提取记忆时经过我们的 AI 代理。详见
  [`PRIVACY.md`](./PRIVACY.md) §3a。
- 心象对话是**情感陪伴体验，不能替代专业的哀伤辅导或心理健康支持**。
  如果你正处于挣扎中，请联系你信任的人或当地心理援助热线。

创建心象时你需勾选确认勾选框，表明已阅读以上内容。违反本节或 §4 使用限制
的账户可能被暂停心象功能。

### 3b. 写信模式（Letter Mode）

"写一封信"功能允许你给某位心象写下一封延迟送达的信。回信由 AI 在送达
窗口到来时生成。

- 这种**延迟感是刻意设计的**：心象的回信**不能被解读为**任何人正在
  "想着你"或"实时回应"——它是由你本机调度的、延迟生成的 AI 文本。
- 回信的语气以**你描述的心象**为准,除你的来信内容与心象已积累的记忆
  之外,不携带任何真实人物的额外信息。
- 写信模式**不适合**用于你想发给真实在世的人的内容。如果你想联系真人,
  请使用真实的沟通渠道。

### 3c. 圆桌（Echo Chamber）

"圆桌" 功能让 3-7 位声音同时回应一个问题,并以「共识 + 分歧 +
下一步问题」三段总结产出。

- **分歧是设计本身,而非缺陷**。请教多位声音的目的就是为了听到
  多个答案;总结部分有意拒绝把不同立场磨平成温和的中间值。
- 圆桌产生的建议依然是 **AI 生成的文本**,适用 §3 同样的免责
  声明 —— 仅供参考,不构成医疗 / 心理 / 法律 / 金融 / 职业建议。
- 当圆桌中包含心象时,该段回应同样使用「这位 X 在你记忆中说过
  的话」的措辞框架(详见 §3a),即创作性诠释,基于**你**的记忆,
  不代表真实人物本人。

### 3d. 跨设备迁移 (Phase 4.5 §E)

跨设备迁移向导帮你把整个金库从一台你的设备搬到另一台。

- 向导生成的 `.vectormigration` 文件包含你在 VECTOR 里所有的内容:
  日记、自定义启明星 / 心象、心象长期记忆、待发送的信件,以及**可选**
  的主密码哈希 + salt。请把它当作 **VECTOR 能产出的最敏感的单个文件**
  来妥善保管和传输。
- 向导显示的 6 位校验码是一个信息性 checksum,**不是**密码学签名。
  它能帮你发现意外损坏 / 选错文件,但**不能**抵御一个同时能改文件
  和能改你看到的码的攻击者。当攻击者模型包含恶意网络时,请使用
  受信任的物理通道 (USB / AirDrop)。
- VECTOR **不**运行任何迁移中转服务器。向导从不上传你的文件。你用
  什么渠道在两台设备之间搬这个文件 (云盘 / 邮件等),那就是**你的**
  渠道 —— 请阅读相应服务方的隐私政策。
- 如果你勾选了携带主密码,新设备就会拥有**和旧设备一样的**主密码。
  当新设备会属于另一个人时,请在导出页**取消勾选**"一并迁移主密码"。

### 3e. 备份完整性 (Phase 4 §4.b-3 — Ed25519 签名)

VECTOR 会用每台设备本地生成的 Ed25519 密钥对**对每一个迁移包做签名**,
并在导入时验证签名。把 §3d 里"校验码不是签名"的免责变成真正的密码学保证。

- 你**首次设置主密码**时,VECTOR 在本地生成一对 Ed25519 密钥。
  公钥作为 16 位指纹(如 `ABCD-EFGH-IJKL-MNOP`)显示在设置里;
  私钥永远不离开你的设备,并以主密码加密的形式存储。
- 迁移导出页在生成签名包后会显示这串指纹。接收设备的导入向导也会
  显示这串指纹,请你确认"这是你另一台设备吗?" —— 只有你确认后,
  向导才会真正写入数据。后续从同一台源设备的导入会跳过这一步
  (TOFU,首次使用即信任)。
- 签名无效会**硬性阻止**导入,这意味着传输中出了 bit 错误,或者
  有攻击者试图替换文件。请回到源设备**重新导出**,而不是忽略错误。
- 设置里的「重新生成设备密钥」会丢弃当前密钥对、生成新的一对。
  当你怀疑本地 IndexedDB 被恶意软件读取过、或在转手设备前,请使用
  此功能。已经信任过旧公钥的接收设备会把新公钥视作"未知",
  重新触发 TOFU 流程。
